# Zero-country recheck — Codex aggregator sweep, 2026-11-14

- **AL:** no longer zero: added `al-gjirokaster`, seasonal, following [DropzoneFinder](https://dropzonefinder.com/dropzones/albania/skydive-albania), [Skydive Albania](https://www.skydivealbania.al/index?lang=en) and [Euronews Albania, 1 May 2026](https://euronews.al/en/50-parachutists-from-italy-open-the-tourist-season-at-the-citys-aerodrome/); repeated aircraft jumps at Gjirokastër airfield are documented.
- **XK:** no current regular civilian dropzone confirmed in Skydiving Source, Skydiving.com, Skyderby or local-language follow-up searches; the opened [CAA parachuting regulation](https://gzk.rks-gov.net/ActDocumentDetail.aspx?ActID=18283) establishes rules, not a place. The opened [24 January 2020 Batllava/Dumosh account](https://www.llapi.info/aeroporti-i-harruar-ne-podujeve/) documents former frequent aircraft jumps and an inactive airfield: `xk-batllava-dumosh` is retained as **uncertain**, with no 2025+ reopening evidence. Kosovo therefore has one historical record and zero confirmed active/seasonal records, rather than a proven absence of all parachuting.

Coordinates for both additions are from the opened [OurAirports dataset](https://davidmegginson.github.io/ourairports-data/airports.csv), identifiers AL-0012 and XK-0009. Both JSON files validate. The existing empty AL/XK containers were populated; no other zero-country report was edited.
