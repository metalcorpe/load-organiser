# Southwestern microstates and territories: aggregator sweep

Codex, checked 2026-11-14. Absence means no qualifying regular aircraft dropzone was confirmed in the opened sources, not proof that no parachute jump has ever occurred. Demonstrations, BASE, paraglider sites and club offices are excluded. No sign-in or operator contact was used.

- **AD — historical recurring site found; populated the previously empty `AD.json`, `ad-borda-mateu`, uncertain.** [RTVA's 17 August 2022 report](https://www.rtva.ad/esports/segona-edicio-andorra-from-above-tindra) documents helicopter jumps into the Borda Mateu meadows in 2019 and 2021 and the planned October 2022 second Andorra from Above edition, including tandem participation. [Municipal club register](https://associacions.andorralavella.ad/node/316) confirms Skydive Andorra la Vella. No 2025+ continuation was established; exact meadow coordinates remain null rather than using the office/stadium address.
- **GI — no qualifying dropzone confirmed; no JSON created.** Checked the UK/Spanish [DropzoneFinder lists](https://dropzonefinder.com/dropzones/united-kingdom/), [Skydiving Source Europe country lists](https://skydivingsource.com/locations/europe/spain/), local-language/search queries and [RAF Gibraltar flying information](https://www.raf.mod.uk/our-organisation/stations/raf-gibraltar/flying-info/). No regular civilian aircraft-jump operator was identified; search leads concerned BASE or charity trips, which do not establish a Gibraltar dropzone.
- **MT — no Maltese operating site confirmed; no JSON created.** [DropzoneFinder Malta](https://dropzonefinder.com/dropzones/malta) has one entry, [The Zoo Skydive Malta](https://dropzonefinder.com/dropzones/malta/the-zoo-skydive-malta), whose own listing says it operates near Pozzallo in Sicily while awaiting a Maltese site. The [October 2026 Malta travel-guide check](https://www.maltaroute.com/things-to-do/active-travel/skydiving-malta/) reaches the same no-confirmed-local-operation conclusion. Neither source identifies an actual Maltese landing field to retain as a historical record.
- **SM — historical directory site found; populated the previously empty `SM.json`, `sm-torraccia`, uncertain.** [DropzoneFinder](https://dropzonefinder.com/dropzones/italy/skydive-san-marino), [Extrevity's explicit Torraccia address](https://extrevity.com/listing/skydive-san-marino/) and [Skydive World](https://www.skydiveworld.com/english/pol_slov.html) list Skydive San Marino. Counter-evidence: the [national Olympic committee's 2012 report](https://www.cons.sm/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/panorama_2012_web2.pdf) describes parachuting abroad and only an annual January demonstration at Torraccia. The record retains this historical directory claim for review; it is not confirmation of a regular or currently operating school. Coordinates are from the opened [LIKD airport page](https://ourairports.com/airports/LIKD/). No 2025+ regular operation found.
- **VA — no qualifying dropzone confirmed; no JSON created.** Checked international directory searches, [Vatican City heliport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vatican_City_Heliport) and Italy directory entries. No aircraft-jump operator or recurring sport/tandem/student landing site was identified. The heliport alone is not a dropzone.
- **MC — no landing site inside Monaco confirmed; no JSON created.** [FFP's Monaco Parachute Team page](https://www.ffp.asso.fr/ou-pratiquer/structures/?code=9810) is a club address. [HelloMonaco's 31 May 2023 account](https://www.hellomonaco.com/news/latest-news/skyfall-the-ultimate-thrill-above-monaco-free-fall-with-skydive-monaco/) describes Heli Air Monaco jumps landing on the beach at Roquebrune-Cap-Martin, France, rather than in Monaco. That French site is an out-of-scope lead for the coordinator; flights above Monaco do not make the landing area MC.
