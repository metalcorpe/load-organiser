## Method and interpretation

Records describe physical airfields or landing areas. Operators sharing an airfield are consolidated. GB includes Northern Ireland and the Isle of Wight; Jersey is JE, so the British federation's Jersey entry is excluded. Its Cyprus entry is also outside scope. Indoor Airkix, university clubs without their own site, offices, brokers and duplicate brands are excluded. “Do a Skydive” points to IPC and is not a second Irish location.

Active means supported by the live federation list checked on the research date, a dated 2025+ federation document or dated operator evidence. This does not establish that a jump took place on the research date. Seasonal is used where a source expressly describes winter closure or summer-only operation. Historical cessation evidence without dated 2025+ confirmation is conservatively classified uncertain, with the old closure/relocation described in the record; thus zero closed records does not mean none of these sites closed. AIP designation alone cannot resolve contradictory suspension evidence.

Coordinates were transcribed from opened airfield pages, airport guides, AIP airfield-centred positions or Mapcarta's mapped features. DMS values were converted arithmetically to decimal degrees. Coordinates indicate the airfield unless a landing area is explicitly named. No postcode or town-centre geocoding was used. Lewknor has null coordinates because the former landing area was not established. Birr's Wikipedia coordinate link has an erroneous “Bantry” label, but the coordinate and airport article are for Birr; this is disclosed in the record.

A single regional Overpass query was attempted for `nwr["sport"="parachuting"](49.8,-11,61,2);out center tags;` at https://overpass-api.de/api/interpreter. The first attempt failed local TLS certificate validation and the second returned HTTP 406. No Overpass result is claimed. Opened Mapcarta OSM-derived pages provided Shotton and the Cockerham landing field; the Movenis page identifies its point as GeoNames. A comprehensive OSM sweep remains a gap.
